# ISO 14001: Environmental Site Inspection

## Standard ID
ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental Management Systems)
Supplementary: EPA Construction General Permit, EU Water Framework Directive

## Scope
Applies to environmental conditions at construction, industrial, and infrastructure sites. Covers water management, soil contamination, vegetation impact, waste management, and drainage assessment.

## Water and Drainage Assessment

### Standing Water
- **Small pooling**: < 1 sq meter area, < 5cm depth. Low risk. Monitor for expansion.
- **Significant pooling**: 1-10 sq meters, 5-15cm depth. Medium risk. Breeding ground for pests within 72 hours. Drainage intervention required.
- **Hazardous accumulation**: > 10 sq meters or > 15cm depth. High risk. Structural flooding potential, safety hazard. Immediate pumping and drainage.

### Drainage System
- **Inlet blockage**: Debris, soil, or vegetation blocking > 50% of inlet = immediate clearing.
- **Outlet erosion**: Visible soil erosion at drainage outfall. Assess slope stability. Silt fence required if sediment load in runoff.
- **Flow direction**: Water must not flow toward structures or excavations. Redirect if necessary.

## Soil and Ground Conditions

### Soil Erosion
- **Sheet erosion**: Uniform soil loss. < 25mm depth = monitor. > 25mm = stabilization required (vegetation, matting, or retaining structure).
- **Rill erosion**: Channel formation. < 50mm depth = fill and regrade. > 50mm = engineered drainage solution.
- **Gully erosion**: Deep channels > 150mm depth. Structural intervention required. Potential slope failure.

### Contamination Indicators
- **Discoloration**: Unusual soil color (blue, green, black) may indicate chemical contamination. Sampling required.
- **Oil sheen**: Rainbow film on water surface. Petroleum contamination. Contain and report to environmental authority.
- **Odor**: Solvent, sulfur, or chemical odors from soil. Stop work. Air quality monitoring required.

## Vegetation and Ecological Impact

### Vegetation Damage
- **Adjacent clearing**: Vegetation removal outside approved work zone = violation. Remediation required.
- **Root zone damage**: Compaction or excavation within tree protection zone (canopy radius + 1m). Tree stability assessment required.
- **Invasive species**: Disturbance may promote invasive plant growth. Monitor and control.

## Waste Management
- **Segregation**: General waste, recyclables, and hazardous waste must be separated. Color-coded containers required.
- **Hazardous waste**: Paints, solvents, batteries, oils must have secondary containment. Manifest for disposal.
- **Debris and rubble**: Construction debris must be in designated containment. Wind-blown debris = litter violation.

## Required Inspection Actions
1. Survey site perimeter for water pooling and drainage flow patterns
2. Inspect all drainage inlets and outlets for blockage
3. Assess soil erosion type and severity
4. Check for contamination indicators (color, sheen, odor)
5. Verify vegetation protection zones are marked and respected
6. Inspect waste segregation and containment
7. Photograph all environmental concerns with location data
8. Generate environmental compliance report with corrective action priorities
