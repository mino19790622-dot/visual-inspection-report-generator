# Road Surface and Traffic Infrastructure Inspection

## Standard ID
ISO 23953: Road condition assessment — Visual and automated inspection methods
Supplementary: AASHTO PP 68, BS EN 13036

## Scope
Applies to road surface condition, traffic signage, road markings, and associated infrastructure. Covers defect classification, measurement methods, and prioritization for maintenance.

## Road Surface Defect Classification

### Potholes
- **Minor**: < 150mm diameter, < 25mm depth. Monitor. Repair within 3 months.
- **Moderate**: 150-600mm diameter, 25-50mm depth. Repair within 1 month. Temporary patch acceptable.
- **Severe**: > 600mm diameter or > 50mm depth. Immediate repair. Traffic management required. Risk of vehicle damage.

### Surface Cracking (Asphalt)
- **Alligator cracking**: Multiple interconnected cracks. Indicates structural failure of base course. Requires full-depth patch.
- **Longitudinal cracking**: Parallel to road centerline. If > 6mm width, crack seal required. Monitor for progression.
- **Transverse cracking**: Across road. Thermal stress related. > 6mm width = seal to prevent water infiltration.

### Surface Deformation
- **Rutting**: > 10mm depth = wheel path depression. Structural failure risk. Requires milling and overlay.
- **Shoving**: Surface bulging. Braking force related. Assess sub-base stability.
- **Settlement**: > 25mm differential = tripping hazard for pedestrians, vehicle impact risk.

## Traffic Sign Condition
- **Retroreflectivity**: Minimum coefficient per ASTM D4956. Replace if below 50% of spec.
- **Legibility**: Must be readable from minimum stopping sight distance at posted speed.
- **Physical damage**: Dents, holes, or bending > 10% of sign face = replacement.
- **Obstruction**: Vegetation or structures blocking > 25% of sign face = immediate clearing.

## Road Marking Condition
- **Visibility**: Day and night visibility required. Retroreflective markings preferred.
- **Wear**: < 50% of marking visible = refresh required. < 25% = immediate repainting.
- **Lane width consistency**: Variance > 50mm from standard = driver confusion risk.

## Required Inspection Actions
1. Drive/walk the full road segment under inspection
2. Photograph all defects with scale and GPS location
3. Measure pothole dimensions (diameter and depth)
4. Assess crack type, width, and extent
5. Check sign retroreflectivity, legibility, and obstruction
6. Evaluate marking visibility and wear
7. Prioritize defects by severity and traffic volume
8. Generate maintenance priority list with timeline
