# Public Transport Vehicle and Stop Safety Inspection

## Standard ID
EN 13134 (Public transport — Safety requirements for bus and coach operations)
Supplementary: UNECE Regulation 107

## Scope
Applies to public transport vehicles (buses, coaches) and associated infrastructure (bus stops, shelters). Covers vehicle condition, passenger safety, stop accessibility, and operational safety.

## Vehicle Condition Assessment

### Exterior
- **Body panels**: No sharp protrusions, exposed edges, or corrosion through panel. Dents > 10cm require body shop assessment.
- **Windows/windshield**: No cracks > 50mm in driver's direct vision. All emergency exits must be functional.
- **Tires**: Minimum tread depth 1.6mm (legal). Recommended 3mm for regular service. Sidewall damage = immediate replacement.
- **Lights**: All exterior lights functional. Headlight alignment within manufacturer spec.
- **Mirrors**: All mirrors intact and correctly positioned. No cracks impairing visibility.

### Interior
- **Handrails/grab poles**: Securely mounted. No sharp edges. All within passenger reach from seated/standing position.
- **Flooring**: No tears > 5cm. Slip-resistant surface maintained. Wheelchair area restraint functional.
- **Seats**: Securely fastened. No broken frames. Fire-retardant material compliant with FMVSS 302.

## Bus Stop Safety Assessment

### Infrastructure
- **Shelter condition**: No broken panels, sharp edges, or structural damage. Roof drainage functional.
- **Boarding area**: Minimum 1.5m clear width. Level surface with no tripping hazards. Tactile paving for visually impaired.
- **Signage**: Route information visible and legible. Stop ID posted. Emergency contact displayed.
- **Lighting**: Adequate illumination for nighttime visibility. No dark spots > 3m from shelter.

### Accessibility
- **Wheelchair access**: Kneeling mechanism functional. Ramp deployment angle < 7 degrees.
- **Priority seating**: Minimum 2 designated spaces. Signage present and unobstructed.
- **Visual/audible announcements**: Next-stop announcements functional. External route display working.

## Pedestrian Interaction
- **Bus stop clearance**: No parked vehicles within 15m of stop. Clear sightline for approaching traffic.
- **Crossing safety**: Pedestrian crossing within 50m preferred. Signalized crossing for high-volume stops.
- **Passenger waiting area**: No encroachment from adjacent land use. Safe set-back from roadway.

## Required Inspection Actions
1. Photograph vehicle exterior (all four sides)
2. Check tire tread depth and condition
3. Test all lights and signals
4. Inspect interior grab rails, flooring, and seating
5. Verify bus stop shelter, signage, and accessibility
6. Assess pedestrian flow and crossing safety
7. Record vehicle/unit number, inspection date, and findings
