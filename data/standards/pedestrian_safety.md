# Pedestrian Safety and Walkway Inspection

## Standard ID
BS EN 1991-1-1 (Pedestrian load and walkway safety)
Supplementary: ADA Accessibility Guidelines, EN 1992-1-1

## Scope
Applies to pedestrian walkways, sidewalks, crossings, and public access areas. Covers surface condition, accessibility, traffic interaction, and personal safety assessment.

## Walkway Surface Condition

### Surface Defects
- **Trip hazards**: Vertical discontinuity > 6mm between adjacent surfaces. Immediate repair required. Risk of fall injury.
- **Cracks**: > 3mm width with vertical displacement = tripping hazard. Fill and level within 7 days.
- **Depression/settlement**: > 10mm depth over 1m span = ponding water hazard. Regrade or patch.
- **Broken/cracked paving**: Loose or missing pavers = trip and fall risk. Replace immediately.

### Surface Condition
- **Slip resistance**: Pendulum test value (PTV) > 36 for dry, > 40 for wet. Below = resurfacing required.
- **Unevenness**: Maximum 1:30 slope ratio for any surface irregularity. Steeper = regrade.
- **Obstruction**: Minimum 1.2m clear width for single pedestrian, 1.8m for two-way traffic.

## Pedestrian-Traffic Interaction

### Road Crossings
- **Marked crossing**: Must have zebra or signal control. Faded markings (< 50% visible) = immediate repainting.
- **Sightline**: Minimum 45m unobstructed view for approaching traffic at 30 km/h. Add 5m per 10 km/h above.
- **Crossing width**: Minimum 2.5m. Must align with walkway direction. Ramps at curb sides.

### Sidewalk-Road Interface
- **Buffer zone**: Preferred 1.5m vegetation buffer between sidewalk and road. Reduces splash and visual separation.
- **Curb condition**: Broken or missing curb > 100mm section = immediate repair. Prevents vehicle encroachment.
- **Bollard/physical barrier**: Where road speed > 50 km/h, physical separation preferred over painted buffer.

## Personal Safety Assessment

### Crowd Density
- **Low**: < 0.5 persons/sq m. Normal flow. No action.
- **Moderate**: 0.5-1.5 persons/sq m. Reduced walking speed. Monitor for bottlenecks.
- **High**: 1.5-3.0 persons/sq m. Congestion risk. Crowd management plan activation.
- **Critical**: > 3.0 persons/sq m. Crowd crush risk. Emergency dispersal required.

### Lighting and Visibility
- **Nighttime illumination**: Minimum 20 lux for pedestrian areas. Dark spots > 3m = security risk.
- **Sightline to exits**: Must be visible from any point in the walkway. No blind corners without mirrors.

### Personnel Counting
- **Group identification**: Identify and count individuals in groups. Note group leaders and vulnerable persons (children, elderly, disabled).
- **Missing person risk**: Groups of 5+ should have appointed leader. Unaccompanied children = welfare concern.
- **PPE in work zones**: Pedestrians in/near construction zones must have hard hat and hi-vis. Non-compliant = removal from zone.

## Required Inspection Actions
1. Walk full length of pedestrian pathway under inspection
2. Measure all surface discontinuities (trip hazards, cracks, settlement)
3. Check slip resistance if equipment available (or note as visual assessment)
4. Verify minimum clear width and identify obstructions
5. Assess crossing markings, sightlines, and curb condition
6. Count and categorize pedestrians (individual, group, vulnerable)
7. Note any persons missing PPE in work zones
8. Evaluate lighting coverage and identify dark spots
9. Generate pedestrian safety report with priority repairs
