# BS EN 1992-1-1: Concrete Structure Crack Assessment

## Standard ID
BS EN 1992-1-1 (Eurocode 2: Design of concrete structures)

## Scope
Applies to visible cracks in concrete structures including buildings, bridges, retaining walls, and pavement surfaces. Covers crack width measurement, severity classification, and required actions.

## Severity Thresholds

### Crack Width
- **Acceptable**: < 0.3 mm — No action required. Monitor during routine inspections.
- **Monitor**: 0.3 mm - 1.0 mm — Document location and width. Schedule follow-up inspection within 6 months. Apply surface sealant if water ingress detected.
- **Repair**: 1.0 mm - 2.0 mm — Immediate structural assessment required. Epoxy injection recommended. Restrict access to affected area until repaired.
- **Critical**: > 2.0 mm — Evacuate area. Emergency structural evaluation. Load-bearing capacity may be compromised. Immediate shoring and repair required.

### Crack Pattern
- **Hairline/random**: Typically shrinkage-related. Low priority.
- **Vertical/parallel**: May indicate settlement. Medium priority. Monitor progression.
- **Diagonal**: Potential shear failure. High priority. Immediate assessment.
- **Horizontal at joints**: Possible reinforcement corrosion. High priority.

## Required Actions
1. Measure crack width using crack gauge or digital caliper
2. Photograph with scale reference
3. Record GPS coordinates and structural element ID
4. Classify severity per thresholds above
5. For Monitor and above, install crack monitoring pins for progression tracking
6. Generate inspection report with recommended remediation timeline
