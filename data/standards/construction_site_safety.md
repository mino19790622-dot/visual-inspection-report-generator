# ISO 45001: Construction Site Safety Inspection

## Standard ID
ISO 45001:2018 (Occupational Health and Safety Management Systems)

## Scope
Applies to active construction, excavation, and infrastructure work sites. Covers PPE compliance, equipment safety zones, personnel safety, and site hazard identification.

## PPE Requirements
- **Hard hat**: Mandatory in all active work zones. Violation = immediate site removal.
- **High-visibility vest**: Required for all personnel. Must meet EN ISO 20471 Class 2 minimum.
- **Safety boots**: Steel-toe (S1 minimum) required. No exceptions for visitors.
- **Eye protection**: Required during cutting, grinding, welding, or chemical handling.

## Equipment Safety Zones
- **Excavator operation radius**: Minimum 5m exclusion zone. Barricades required.
- **Dump truck loading/unloading**: 10m exclusion zone. Ground spotter mandatory.
- **Overhead lifting operations**: Entire crane swing radius is exclusion zone. No personnel under loads.
- **Pipe storage/staging**: Stable stacking required. Maximum stack height: 2m. Dunnage between layers.

## Hazard Assessment Thresholds
- **Standing water**: > 10cm depth = slip/fall hazard. Drainage or barricade required within 24 hours.
- **Unstable soil/slope**: Slope angle > 30 degrees with loose soil = collapse risk. Shoring or benching required.
- **Missing safety barriers**: Any unprotected edge > 1.5m height = immediate violation.
- **Equipment proximity to structures**: < 2m clearance = risk of structural damage. Spotter required.

## Personnel Accounting
- All sites must maintain real-time personnel count.
- Daily sign-in/sign-out log mandatory.
- Headcount verification required at shift change and emergency evacuation.
- Missing person = stop work, immediate search, notify safety officer.

## Required Inspection Actions
1. Verify PPE compliance for all visible personnel
2. Check exclusion zones are barricaded and signed
3. Inspect equipment positioning relative to personnel and structures
4. Assess ground conditions (water, soil stability, debris)
5. Verify safety barriers and edge protection
6. Document violations with photographs and corrective action timeline
